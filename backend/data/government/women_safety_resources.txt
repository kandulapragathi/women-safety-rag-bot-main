WOMEN SAFETY AND SUPPORT RESOURCES - INDIA

This document contains basic safety and support information for women in India.

Emergency:
For immediate emergencies in India, the emergency response number is 112.

Women Helpline:
The Women Helpline number is 181. It provides support and can help connect women with appropriate services.

Safety Guidance:
If a person is in immediate danger, move to a safer location when possible and contact emergency services.

Privacy:
Do not share unnecessary personal information with an unknown person or service.

Important:
The chatbot should not invent helpline numbers, legal procedures, organizations, or other safety information. When information is unavailable in the knowledge base, the chatbot should clearly say that it does not have the information.
