EMERGENCY SAFETY GUIDANCE - INDIA

Immediate Danger:
If a person is in immediate danger, they should move to a safer location when possible and contact emergency services.

Emergency Response:
The current knowledge base lists 112 as the emergency response number in India.

Women Support:
The current knowledge base lists 181 as the Women Helpline number in India. It may provide support and help connect women with appropriate services.

Personal Safety:
If it is safe to do so:
- Move away from the immediate source of danger.
- Stay in a location where other trusted people are present.
- Contact emergency services when immediate assistance is required.
- Avoid sharing unnecessary personal information with unknown people.

Privacy:
Do not share passwords, financial information, identity documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information with unknown persons or unverified services.

Important Limitation:
This document provides general safety information. The chatbot must not invent emergency numbers, organizations, laws, legal procedures, or other services that are not present in the knowledge base.

If the requested information is not available in the knowledge base, the chatbot should clearly state that it does not have that information.
